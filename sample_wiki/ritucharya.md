Ritucharya (ऋतुचर्या, "seasonal conduct") is the Ayurvedic science of adapting diet (Ahara), lifestyle (Vihara), and daily practices to the six Hindu seasons (Ritus). It operates on the core principle that health is a dynamic equilibrium between the body's internal energies (Doshas) and external environmental forces, maintained through conscious seasonal adjustment. Ritucharya transforms the [[panchanga]] from a time-keeping system into a proactive wellness protocol. [Source: Hindu_Months_Health_and_Wellness_full.md]

## Season-by-Season Protocol

### Vasanta (Spring) — Kapha Pacification

**Ahara (Diet):** Favor light, dry, warm foods. Emphasize Katu (pungent), Tikta (bitter), Kashaya (astringent) tastes. Old barley, wheat, lentils, honey. Avoid heavy, oily, cold, sweet, sour foods; new grains; excessive dairy. [Source: Hindu_Months_Health_and_Wellness_full.md]

**Vihara (Lifestyle):** Vigorous exercise. Udvartana (dry powder massage). Kavala (gargling) and Nasya (nasal cleansing). No daytime sleeping. [Source: Hindu_Months_Health_and_Wellness_full.md]

### Grishma (Summer) — Pitta & Vata Management

**Ahara:** Sweet, light, cold, liquid foods. Water, fresh juices, buttermilk. Rice, lentils, cooling vegetables. Avoid hot, spicy, pungent, sour, salty foods. Minimal alcohol. [Source: Hindu_Months_Health_and_Wellness_full.md]

**Vihara:** Stay cool, avoid midday sun. Light clothing. Sandalwood paste. Short daytime nap permitted. Gentle exercise only. [Source: Hindu_Months_Health_and_Wellness_full.md]

### Varsha (Monsoon) — Vata Pacification & Agni Protection

**Ahara:** Sour, salty, unctuous foods. Old grains, soups, digestive spices (ginger, pepper, hing). Boiled water. Avoid heavy foods, leafy greens, raw salads, excess liquids, dairy. [Source: Hindu_Months_Health_and_Wellness_full.md]

**Vihara:** Keep surroundings dry. Warm water bathing. Abhyanga (oil massage). Basti (medicated enemas) — primary Panchakarma for Vata. Avoid rain exposure, daytime sleep, cold winds. [Source: Hindu_Months_Health_and_Wellness_full.md]

### Sharad (Autumn) — Pitta Pacification

**Ahara:** Sweet, bitter tastes; light, cooling foods. Wheat, green gram, ghee, pumpkin, gourds. Hansodaka (sun- and moon-purified water). Avoid hot, sour, salty foods; fats; curd. [Source: Hindu_Months_Health_and_Wellness_full.md]

**Vihara:** Eat only when hungry. Moonlight exposure. Light clothes, cooling flower garlands. Virechana (purgation) and Rakta Mokshana (bloodletting) as Panchakarma. No daytime sleeping. [Source: Hindu_Months_Health_and_Wellness_full.md]

### Hemanta (Pre-Winter) — Nourishment Season

**Ahara:** Heavy, nourishing, unctuous foods. Sweet, sour, salty tastes. New rice, wheat, black gram, milk products, jaggery, sesame oil, meat soups. Avoid light, cold, dry foods. [Source: Hindu_Months_Health_and_Wellness_full.md]

**Vihara:** Vigorous exercise, Abhyanga, head massage. Sunbathing. Warm water, warm clothing. Sexual activity permitted (strength is high). Avoid cold winds. [Source: Hindu_Months_Health_and_Wellness_full.md]

### Shishira (Winter) — Continuation of Hemanta

**Ahara:** Same as Hemanta — warm, oily, nourishing. Cereals, pulses, ginger, garlic, Chyavanprash. Avoid pungent, bitter, astringent foods. [Source: Hindu_Months_Health_and_Wellness_full.md]

**Vihara:** Same as Hemanta. Protect from cold winds. Morning sun exposure. No fasting (increases Kapha). No daytime sleep. [Source: Hindu_Months_Health_and_Wellness_full.md]

## The Personalization Triad

General Ritucharya applies to everyone, but true optimization requires personalizing at the intersection of three factors:

1. **Ritu** (Season) — the environmental influence
2. **Prakriti** (innate constitution) — the individual's lifelong Vata-Pitta-Kapha balance
3. **Vikriti** (current imbalance) — the present deviation from baseline

A Pitta-Prakriti person needs extra cooling vigilance in Grishma; a Vata-Prakriti person needs extra grounding in Varsha. This personalization logic is central to NeoRishi's recommendation engine. [Source: Hindu_Months_Health_and_Wellness_full.md]

The Adana-Visarga Kala framework anticipates modern findings that human physiology follows a semi-annual cycle of depletion and restoration. [Source: Hindu_Months_Health_and_Wellness_full.md]

## Ritucharya and the Pancha Kosha Model

Ritucharya primarily operates at the **Annamaya** (physical body — nutrition, exercise) and **Pranamaya** (vital energy — breathing, nervous system) kosha levels. The [[pancha-kosha]] framework positions Ritucharya as the seasonal maintenance protocol for the outermost two sheaths, which serve as the foundation enabling inner kosha work (Manomaya, Vijnanamaya, Anandamaya). Seasonal imbalance at the Annamaya-Pranamaya level (e.g., Vata aggravation causing nervous system dysregulation in Varsha) can cascade inward, disrupting emotional stability (Manomaya) and decision quality (Vijnanamaya). [Source: pancha_kosha_framework.md]

## Seasonal Regimen — Detailed Protocols

Ritucharya (Ritu = season, Charya = regimen/conduct) is the Ayurvedic system of seasonal dietary and lifestyle adaptation designed to maintain Dosha balance across nature's cycles. Based on the axiom "like increases like, opposites balance" (Samanya-Vishesha Siddhanta), seasons with qualities similar to a Dosha will increase that Dosha. By proactively adopting opposing qualities, seasonal disease is prevented. Ritucharya is primarily a **preventive strategy** — it intercepts Dosha accumulation at [[shatkriyakala|Stage 1 (Sanchaya)]] before clinical disease can develop. [Source: Tridosha_Principle_Comprehensive_Guide.md]

## The Six Seasons (Ritus)

| Season | Sanskrit | Approx. Months | Dominant Qualities | Dosha Accumulation | Dosha Aggravation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Late Winter | Shishira | mid-Jan to mid-Mar | Cold, dry | **Kapha** | Vata (if very dry/windy) |
| Spring | Vasanta | mid-Mar to mid-May | Warm, moist, soft | — | **Kapha** (liquefies) |
| Summer | Grishma | mid-May to mid-Jul | Hot, dry, sharp | **Vata** (dryness) | **Pitta** (heat) |
| Monsoon | Varsha | mid-Jul to mid-Sep | Cold, damp, windy, unstable | **Pitta** (acidic) | **Vata** (cold/wind) |
| Autumn | Sharad | mid-Sep to mid-Nov | Hot (initially), dry, clear | — | **Pitta** (returns) |
| Early Winter | Hemanta | mid-Nov to mid-Jan | Cold, damp, heavy | **Kapha** | — |

[Source: Tridosha_Principle_Comprehensive_Guide.md]

## The Lag Effect — Predictive Intervention Window

Doshas follow a three-phase seasonal cycle:
1. **Chaya** (Accumulation) — Dosha builds up gradually
2. **Prakopa** (Aggravation) — Dosha flares in a subsequent season
3. **Prashama** (Pacification) — Dosha subsides naturally

The accumulation happens in one season, but aggravation manifests in the NEXT season. This creates a **predictive intervention window**: by implementing pacifying measures during the Chaya phase, aggravation can be entirely prevented. Example: introducing warming Vata-pacifying foods at the end of summer prevents Vata aggravation in the coming monsoon. [Source: Tridosha_Principle_Comprehensive_Guide.md]

## Seasonal Protocols

### Vata Season (Late Fall / Early Winter — cold, dry, windy)
- **Diet**: Warm, moist, nourishing, grounding; favour sweet, sour, salty tastes; cooked grains, soups, stews, root vegetables, nuts, ghee, sesame oil
- **Avoid**: Cold, raw, dry, light foods (salads, iced drinks, crackers); pungent, bitter, astringent tastes
- **Lifestyle**: Consistent daily routine; stay warm; Abhyanga with warm sesame oil; gentle grounding exercise (yoga, walking); adequate rest

### Pitta Season (Summer / Early Autumn — hot, intense)
- **Diet**: Cooling, refreshing, slightly moist; favour sweet, bitter, astringent; fresh fruits, cucumbers, leafy greens, coconut, barley
- **Avoid**: Hot, spicy, oily, fried, fermented; sour, salty, pungent; alcohol
- **Lifestyle**: Stay cool; avoid midday sun (10 AM-2 PM); swimming, nature walks; Shitali pranayama; coconut oil Abhyanga

### Kapha Season (Late Winter / Spring — cold, damp, heavy)
- **Diet**: Light, dry, warming; favour pungent, bitter, astringent; steamed vegetables, legumes, barley, millet, warming spices, honey
- **Avoid**: Heavy, oily, cold, sweet, sour, salty; dairy, fried foods, cold drinks
- **Lifestyle**: Regular vigorous exercise (especially before 10 AM); avoid daytime sleep; stay warm/dry; dry brushing (Garshana); Abhyanga with warming oils

[Source: Tridosha_Principle_Comprehensive_Guide.md]

## Ritu Sandhi — Seasonal Transitions

The last 7 days of the outgoing season and first 7 days of the incoming season form the **Ritu Sandhi** (seasonal junction). During this period:
- Gradually taper off the previous season's regimen
- Slowly introduce practices for the approaching season
- This gradual adaptation allows Agni and physiology to adjust smoothly, preventing imbalances from abrupt changes

[Source: Tridosha_Principle_Comprehensive_Guide.md]

## Dosha-Aligned Circadian Protocol (Dinacharya)

Within each day, Doshas cycle in 4-hour blocks:

| Time | Dominant Dosha | Optimal Activities | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6-10 AM | Kapha | Vigorous exercise, complex mental work, lighter meals | Heavy meals, sleeping in, inactivity |
| 10 AM-2 PM | Pitta | **Largest meal**, focused analytical work, decision-making | Excessive sun, arguments, skipping lunch |
| 2-6 PM | Vata | Creative work, light movement, social connection | Major decisions under fatigue, caffeine |
| 6-10 PM | Kapha | Light dinner, calming activities, early sleep prep | Heavy eating, screens, stimulating content |
| 10 PM-2 AM | Pitta | **Deep sleep** (nocturnal tissue repair/detox) | Staying awake, eating, screens |
| 2-6 AM | Vata | Deep/REM sleep, dreaming, pre-dawn meditation | Interrupted sleep, alarm-driven waking |

[Source: dosha_dushya_framework.md, Tridosha_Principle_Comprehensive_Guide.md]

> BRIDGE: Dosha circadian rhythms ↔ Modern chronobiology: asthma attacks (Kapha-Pranavahasrotas) peak in Kapha time; acid reflux (Pitta-Annavahasrotas) peaks in Pitta time; matching circadian regulation of inflammatory cytokines, cortisol, and melatonin. [Source: dosha_dushya_framework.md]

## Seasonal Lifestyle Adaptations

Ritucharya Lifestyle extends the dietary seasonal regimen documented in [[ritucharya]] to cover the full spectrum of non-dietary lifestyle adaptations: clothing, bathing protocols, sleep duration, home environment, social engagement, sexual activity, exercise, and travel — all modulated by season and the principle of *Samanya-Vishesha Siddhanta* ("like increases like; opposites pacify"). This page is the operational Vihara complement to the Ahara-focused [[ritucharya]].

## 1. Bala (Strength) Fluctuation Across the Year

### The Two Ayanas

**Aadaan Kaal (Draining Period): Mid-January to Mid-July** — [[ritu-dosha-mapping|Uttarayana]]. Increasing heat, dryness, intensity. Human strength progressively declines. Seasons: Shishira, Vasanta, Grishma. [Source: ritucharya-lifestyle.md]

**Visarga Kaal (Nourishing Period): Mid-July to Mid-January** — [[ritu-dosha-mapping|Dakshinayana]]. Cooling, moistening, nurturing. Human strength progressively increases. Seasons: Varsha, Sharad, Hemanta. [Source: ritucharya-lifestyle.md]

### Bala Across Six Seasons

| Season | Period | Ayana | Bala Level | State |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[hemanta-ritu\|Hemanta]] | Nov 15-Jan 15 | Visarga | **Peak** | Strong Agni, peak digestion |
| [[shishira-ritu\|Shishira]] | Jan 15-Mar 15 | Aadaan | High | Strong Agni; Kapha accumulating |
| [[vasanta-ritu\|Vasanta]] | Mar 15-May 15 | Aadaan | Moderate | Weakening Agni; Kapha Prakopa |
| [[grishma-ritu\|Grishma]] | May 15-Jul 15 | Aadaan | **Minimum** | Weak Agni; Vata accumulating |
| [[varsha-ritu\|Varsha]] | Jul 15-Sep 15 | Visarga | Low | Weak Agni; Vata Prakopa |
| [[sharad-ritu\|Sharad]] | Sep 15-Nov 15 | Visarga | Moderate | Recovering Agni; Pitta Prakopa |

[Source: ritucharya-lifestyle.md]

## 2. Hemanta Ritu: Pre-Winter (Mid-November to Mid-January)

**Environmental qualities:** Cool, stable, reduced wind, clear skies, invigorating cold. [Source: ritucharya-lifestyle.md]

| Domain | Protocol |
|---|---|
| **Clothing** | Heavy wools, silks, leather; warm tones (browns, reds); hats, gloves, scarves; insulated footwear |
| **Bathing** | Warm to hot water; daily; warm sesame oil Abhyanga before bathing; avoid rapid post-bath temperature drop |
| **Sleep** | 8-9 hours; bedtime 10:00 PM; wake 5:30-6:00 AM; strictly no daytime naps; warm, draft-free bedroom |
| **Home** | 68-72°F (20-22°C); warm aromas (sandalwood, frankincense); warm artificial lighting evenings |
| **Social** | Moderate to high; season of celebration; shared meals encouraged |
| **Sexual Activity** | Encouraged (body's high strength supports vitality expenditure) |
| **Exercise** | Vigorous, heating; strength training; 30-45 min moderate-to-vigorous; morning 6:30-8:00 AM |
| **Travel** | Sun exposure beneficial; minimize wind exposure; protect against cold |

[Source: ritucharya-lifestyle.md]

## 3. Shishira Ritu: Winter (Mid-January to Mid-March)

**Environmental qualities:** Peak cold and dryness, strong winds, variable weather, low humidity. [Source: ritucharya-lifestyle.md]

| Domain | Protocol |
|---|---|
| **Clothing** | Heaviest layers (3-4); dense wools, insulated synthetics; waterproof footwear; ear/nose/finger protection crucial |
| **Bathing** | Hot water (warmest of all seasons); herbal additions (ginger, turmeric, cinnamon); immediate wrapping post-bath |
| **Sleep** | 8-9 hours; left-side sleeping favors warmth retention; daytime napping strictly prohibited (increases Kapha) |
| **Home** | 70-74°F (21-23°C); minimal ventilation; warming aromas (sandalwood, clove, black pepper); morning sunbathing through windows |
| **Social** | Moderate; more home-based; monitor for seasonal affective patterns |
| **Sexual Activity** | Encouraged (high strength and warmth) |
| **Exercise** | Vigorous, warming; dynamic yoga, strength training; 30-45 min high intensity; indoors if windy |
| **Travel** | Sun exposure especially beneficial; mandatory wind protection; avoid early morning commute (coldest) |

[Source: ritucharya-lifestyle.md]

## 4. Vasanta Ritu: Spring (Mid-March to Mid-May)

**Environmental qualities:** Increasing warmth, high humidity, pollen, variable (warm days/cold nights). [Source: ritucharya-lifestyle.md]

| Domain | Protocol |
|---|---|
| **Clothing** | Lighter layers (2-3); cotton blends, lighter wools; lighter tones (whites, pastels); adjustable for variable weather |
| **Bathing** | Warm (not hot); **Udvartana (dry powder massage)** preferred over oil — sandalwood, saffron, chickpea flour; reduces heavy Kapha quality |
| **Sleep** | 7-8 hours (less than winter); daytime napping **absolutely prohibited** (aggravates Kapha) |
| **Home** | Allow cooling (68-70°F); increase ventilation; dry environment preferred; cooling aromas (lemongrass, peppermint, eucalyptus) |
| **Social** | High; outdoor activities, hiking, nature walks; season of festivals |
| **Sexual Activity** | Moderate |
| **Exercise** | **Vigorous, stimulating, weight-reducing** (Kapha liquefaction emphasis); brisk walking, running, vigorous yoga, martial arts; 30-60 min; outdoor exercise optimal |
| **Travel** | Outdoor travel excellent; nature immersion strongly encouraged |

[Source: ritucharya-lifestyle.md]

## 5. Grishma Ritu: Summer (Mid-May to Mid-July)

**Environmental qualities:** Intense heat, low humidity, dried environment. Bala at **minimum**. [Source: ritucharya-lifestyle.md]

| Domain | Protocol |
|---|---|
| **Clothing** | Lightest possible (cotton, linen, loose); pale/reflective colors (white, cream); sun-protective but breathable |
| **Bathing** | Cool water (not cold); **twice daily** standard; cooling herbs (sandalwood, rose water); light coconut oil or none after |
| **Sleep** | 7-8 hours; **unique exception: short 20-30 min nap permitted** (energy restoration during heat stress, 2-3 PM); maximum cooling in sleep environment |
| **Home** | Cool maintained (75-78°F); maximum air circulation; minimize sunlight during peak (9 AM-4 PM); cooling aromas (rose, jasmine, sandalwood) |
| **Social** | Reduced; shift to early morning (before 8 AM) and late evening (after 7 PM); water-based activities encouraged |
| **Sexual Activity** | Minimize (energy-depleting during heat stress) |
| **Exercise** | **Gentle, non-heating, cooling** (yin yoga, slow walking, swimming); well below half-strength (quarter-strength or less); 20-30 min max; early morning or late evening only |
| **Travel** | Minimize sun exposure; avoid midday entirely; constant hydration; early morning or late evening only |

[Source: ritucharya-lifestyle.md]

## 6. Varsha Ritu: Monsoon (Mid-July to Mid-September)

**Environmental qualities:** High humidity, frequent rainfall, cloudy, cool-damp, acidic vapor. [Source: ritucharya-lifestyle.md]

| Domain | Protocol |
|---|---|
| **Clothing** | Moisture-wicking cotton; quick-dry fabrics; warm tones; full coverage; waterproof footwear with antifungal treatment |
| **Bathing** | Warm water (counteract damp chill); use boiled water; Abhyanga before bathing to pacify [[vata-dosha\|Vata]]; thorough drying essential |
| **Sleep** | 7-8 hours; **absolutely no daytime naps** (depletes weak Agni); moisture control in bedding; dehumidifier if needed |
| **Home** | Warm (70-72°F); **moisture control critical** (dehumidifiers); rigorous cleanliness (fungal/microbial risk); burn antimicrobial herbs (neem, guggulu) |
| **Social** | Moderate to low; indoor activities preferred; avoid natural water bodies (contamination); minimize travel |
| **Sexual Activity** | Minimize (body's strength is low; Vata aggravated) |
| **Exercise** | **Light, warming, Vata-pacifying** (gentle yoga, slow walking, tai chi); 20-30 min max; indoors or covered areas |
| **Travel** | Minimize rain exposure; avoid waterlogged areas; avoid dew exposure; postpone non-essential journeys |

[Source: ritucharya-lifestyle.md]

## 7. Sharad Ritu: Autumn (Mid-September to Mid-November)

**Environmental qualities:** Clear bright skies, intense but declining sun, reduced humidity, cool evenings. [Source: ritucharya-lifestyle.md]

| Domain | Protocol |
|---|---|
| **Clothing** | Moderate weight (cotton, light silks); cooling tones (whites, blues); 1-2 layers; warmer evening layer |
| **Bathing** | Cool water; cooling herbal pastes (sandalwood, vetiver) before bathing; cool evening bath beneficial |
| **Sleep** | 6-8 hours; daytime napping **absolutely prohibited** (disrupts Pitta recovery); well-ventilated, cool environment |
| **Home** | Slightly cool (70°F); open, good airflow; cooling aromas (sandalwood, vetiver, rose, lotus); air purification for dust |
| **Social** | Moderate to high; morning and late evening optimal; avoid midday heat; **evening socializing encouraged (moon exposure beneficial for [[pitta-dosha\|Pitta]])** |
| **Sexual Activity** | Moderate |
| **Exercise** | Moderate, cooling (swimming, cycling, moderate yoga); 30-45 min; early morning or late evening; avoid vigorous heat-generating activity |
| **Travel** | Minimize peak-hour sun; **Chandani Sevana (moon bathing) encouraged** — spend time in moonlight first 3 hours of night; water activities encouraged |

[Source: ritucharya-lifestyle.md]

## 8. Ritu Sandhi: The 14-Day Seasonal Transition Protocol

**Ritu Sandhi** (seasonal junction) = last 7 days of outgoing season + first 7 days of incoming season. This 14-day window creates physiological stress. Abrupt changes trigger *Asatmyaja Roga* (intolerance diseases). [Source: ritucharya-lifestyle.md]

### Gradual Dietary Shift Protocol

| Days | Old Season % | New Season % |
|---|---|---|
| Days 1-2 | 85% | 15% |
| Days 3-4 | 70% | 30% |
| Days 5-6 | 55% | 45% |
| Days 7-8 | 40% | 60% |
| Days 9-10 | 25% | 75% |
| Days 11-12 | 10% | 90% |
| Days 13-14 | 0% | 100% |

[Source: ritucharya-lifestyle.md]

### Lifestyle Transition Guidance
- **Clothing:** Gradual adjustment (remove layers slowly; don't switch abruptly)
- **Exercise:** Gradual intensity change (increase or decrease 10-20% every 2-3 days)
- **Sleep:** Maintain consistency; don't shift dramatically
- **Bathing:** Adjust water temperature gradually over the 14 days

[Source: ritucharya-lifestyle.md]

### Environmental Awareness
- Don't follow calendar rigidly; **watch actual weather**
- Bud emergence signals spring more accurately than March 15
- First frost signals Shishira more accurately than January 15
- "Seasons are not equally felt in all places" — adapt timing to local climate

[Source: ritucharya-lifestyle.md]
