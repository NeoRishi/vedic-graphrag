Dinacharya Rules provides the granular, step-by-step daily routine protocol from Brahma Muhurta through nighttime sleep, with dosha-specific variations for every practice, modern science correlates, and direct VBR Engine integration points. This page is the operational deep-dive companion to [[dinacharya]], which provides the conceptual overview.

## 1. Brahma Muhurta: The Awakening Window

**Timing:** 96 minutes before sunrise (approximately 4:00-5:30 AM depending on latitude and season). Wake during the final [[vata-dosha|Vata]]-dominant phase of the night. [Source: dinacharya-rules.md]

**Physiological Rationale:** During Brahma Muhurta, cortisol begins its pre-dawn surge, reaching 50-60% of daily peak within the first 30 minutes. Waking at this time avoids jarring alarm-induced cortisol spikes, synchronizes with Vata's natural movement properties (facilitating elimination), captures the sattvic quality of pre-dawn atmosphere, and establishes optimal HPA-axis function. [Source: dinacharya-rules.md]

> BRIDGE: Brahma Muhurta awakening ↔ Cortisol Awakening Response (CAR) literature confirms that consistent early wake timing produces robust CAR with superior immune surveillance, cognitive performance, and metabolic health throughout the day. [Source: dinacharya-rules.md]

## 2. Morning Cleansing Rituals (6:00-8:00 AM)

### 2.1 Mala Tyaga (Elimination)
Attend to natural urges immediately upon waking. The pre-dawn Vata window facilitates high-amplitude propagating contractions (HAPCs) — natural mass-movement peristalsis triggered by the sleep-to-wake transition. Never suppress natural urges (*vegas*); this is foundational preventive medicine per Charaka Samhita. [Source: dinacharya-rules.md]

### 2.2 Danta Dhavana (Tooth Cleansing)
Clean teeth thoroughly immediately after elimination, before eating. Removes overnight bacterial biofilm and reduces lipopolysaccharides before they enter the GI tract. [Source: dinacharya-rules.md]

> BRIDGE: Oral-systemic axis research confirms that Porphyromonas gingivalis and periodontal pathogens correlate with systemic inflammation markers (CRP, IL-6) and atherosclerotic plaque development. [Source: dinacharya-rules.md]

### 2.3 Jihva Nirlekhana (Tongue Scraping)
Scrape tongue gently with a metal scraper (U-shaped, copper or stainless steel), 5-7 strokes from root to tip. Removes Ama (metabolic endotoxin) visible as coating, bacterial biofilm, and volatile sulfur compounds. [Source: dinacharya-rules.md]

### 2.4 Gandoosha (Oil Pulling)
Hold oil in mouth for 3-5 minutes. The lipophilic medium extracts fat-soluble bacterial metabolites, reduces bacterial load, and activates palatal mechanoreceptors (parasympathetic activation). [Source: dinacharya-rules.md]

**Oils by constitution:**

| Constitution | Oil | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| [[vata-dosha\|Vata]] | Warm sesame oil | Warming, grounding |
| [[pitta-dosha\|Pitta]] | Coconut oil | Cooling |
| [[kapha-dosha\|Kapha]] | Mustard oil | Stimulating |

[Source: dinacharya-rules.md]

### 2.5 Anjana (Eye Anointing)
Apply thin line of ghee, sesame oil, or Ayurvedic eye preparation along the inner eyelid. Tones eye muscles, prevents dryness, enhances visual acuity. [Source: dinacharya-rules.md]

### 2.6 Nasya (Nasal Administration)
Instill 3-5 drops of warm oil into each nostril. Lubricates nasal passages, removes sinus toxins, supports lymphatic drainage, prevents Vata-aggravating dryness. [Source: dinacharya-rules.md]

**Constitutional Variations:**
- **Vata:** Warm sesame oil
- **Pitta:** Warm coconut oil
- **Kapha:** Dry inhalation of warming spice powder instead

[Source: dinacharya-rules.md]

## 3. Abhyanga (Oil Self-Massage)

**Timing:** Early morning, 6:00-7:00 AM, after elimination and cleansing. Duration: 10-20 minutes daily; 20-30 minutes for weekend practice. Minimum 3-4 times per week. [Source: dinacharya-rules.md]

### Oil Selection by Constitution

| Constitution | Oil | Temperature |
|---|---|---|
| [[vata-dosha\|Vata]] | Sesame | Warm (not hot) |
| [[pitta-dosha\|Pitta]] | Coconut | Room temp or slightly cool |
| [[kapha-dosha\|Kapha]] | Mustard or Neem | Warm |
| Vata-Pitta | Sesame-Coconut blend | Lukewarm |

[Source: dinacharya-rules.md]

### Technique and Direction
Sequence: Head (Shiro-Abhyanga, circular) → Face (upward strokes) → Neck (downward strokes) → Arms (long strokes shoulder to wrist, circular at joints) → Chest/Torso (circular over heart, long strokes over ribs) → Abdomen (clockwise following colon path) → Back (long strokes along spine, never directly on spine) → Legs (long strokes hip to ankle, circular at joints) → Feet (Pada-Abhyanga, vigorous soles and between toes). [Source: dinacharya-rules.md]

### Physiological Mechanisms

> BRIDGE: Abhyanga physiological mechanisms ↔ Modern research: (1) PPAR activation — cutaneous lipid application activates peroxisome proliferator-activated receptors, modulating local inflammatory cascades; (2) Lymphatic stimulation — external compression activates lymph circulation (which lacks intrinsic pump); (3) Vagal afferent activation — cutaneous touch stimulates vagal nerve fibers, enhancing parasympathetic tone; (4) Fascial remodeling — regular massage maintains fascial compliance and neural density in skin. [Source: dinacharya-rules.md]

Allow 5-10 minutes for oil absorption before bathing. Oil acts as joint lubricant during subsequent exercise. [Source: dinacharya-rules.md]

## 4. Vyayama (Exercise)

**Timing:** 6:30-8:00 AM (Kapha-dominant period). Duration: 20-45 minutes depending on constitution and season. [Source: dinacharya-rules.md]

### The Ardha Shakti (Half-Strength) Principle
Classical texts prescribe exercise at no more than 50% of full capacity. This maps to the ventilatory threshold — the aerobic-anaerobic boundary — optimizing metabolic outcomes without depleting [[pancha-kosha|Ojas]] (vitality reserve) and preventing Vata-aggravating over-exercise. See [[exercise-by-dosha]] for complete constitutional exercise prescriptions. [Source: dinacharya-rules.md]

**Types by Constitution:**
- **Vata:** Slow yoga, walking on flat terrain, swimming, Tai Chi. Avoid jumping, high-impact.
- **Pitta:** Swimming, cycling, moderate cooling yoga. Avoid running in heat, competitive sports.
- **Kapha:** Brisk walking, Sun Salutations, vigorous yoga, strength training. Avoid sedentary practices.

[Source: dinacharya-rules.md]

**Post-Exercise:** Rest 5-10 minutes in cool shade. Sip warm herbal tea or plain warm water. Avoid immediate cold bathing (wait 15-30 minutes). [Source: dinacharya-rules.md]

## 5. Snana (Bathing)

**Timing:** 30-45 minutes post-exercise. [Source: dinacharya-rules.md]

**Temperature by constitution:**
- **Vata:** Warm water
- **Pitta:** Cool (not cold) water
- **Kapha:** Hot water

Removes oil and mobilized waste. Completes thermoregulation post-exercise. Provides psychological reset before entering work mode. [Source: dinacharya-rules.md]

## 7. Ratricharya: Evening and Night Routine

### 6:00-7:00 PM: Early Evening Transition
Reduce intensity; shift to lighter tasks. Begin dimming lighting (supporting melatonin production). Transition from work to home; gratitude or reflection practice. [Source: dinacharya-rules.md]

### 7:00-8:00 PM: Dinner
Light, warm, easily digestible: warm soups, steamed vegetables, light grains, herbal teas. Avoid heavy proteins, cold foods, caffeine, stimulating spices. Eat by 7:30 PM at latest; minimum 3 hours before sleep. [Source: dinacharya-rules.md]

### 8:00-9:00 PM: Evening Wind-Down
Gentle stretching or restorative yoga. Herbal tea preparations by dosha:
- **Vata:** Warm milk with Ashwagandha, ghee, honey
- **Pitta:** Warm milk with Brahmi (Bacopa), rose petal water
- **Kapha:** Ginger or cinnamon tea; avoid dairy

Journaling, light reading, meaningful conversation. Gradual reduction of sensory stimulation. [Source: dinacharya-rules.md]

### 9:00-10:00 PM: Sleep Preparation

**Pada-Abhyanga (Foot Massage):** Vigorous oil massage of feet (5-10 minutes). Feet contain dense cutaneous nerve endings; stimulation activates parasympathetic pathways. Oils: sesame (Vata), coconut (Pitta), mustard (Kapha). [Source: dinacharya-rules.md]

**Bedroom:** Temperature 16-19°C (60-66°F). Complete darkness. Silence or white noise. Aromas: lavender, sandalwood, jasmine. Natural fiber bedding. [Source: dinacharya-rules.md]

**Pre-Sleep Ritual:** Warm bath (10-15 min) → Oil massage → Coherent breathing (5.5 breaths/min, 5 min) or left-nostril breathing → Meditation or body scan (5-10 min) → Settle into bed by 9:45-10:00 PM. [Source: dinacharya-rules.md]

### Sleep Timing by Constitution

| Constitution | Optimal Window | Duration | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vata | 10:00 PM-6:00 AM | 7-8 hours | Needs consistency; benefits from slightly longer sleep |
| Pitta | 10:00 PM-6:00 AM | 7-8 hours | Can tolerate slight activity before bed |
| Kapha | 10:00 PM-6:00 AM | 6-7 hours | Excess sleep increases sluggishness |

See [[sleep-protocols]] for comprehensive dosha-specific sleep protocols. [Source: dinacharya-rules.md]

### Critical: Sleep Before 10 PM
Remaining awake past 10 PM triggers nocturnal Pitta (the "second wind"): increased alertness, reactivation of appetite, diversion of Pitta's energy from tissue repair to external consciousness. Creates sleep phase delay and circadian misalignment. [Source: dinacharya-rules.md]

> BRIDGE: The "forbidden zone for sleep" — dim-light melatonin onset (DLMO) typically occurs around 9-10 PM; sleep initiation is most efficient 1-2 hours after DLMO. [Source: dinacharya-rules.md]

### Divaswapna (Daytime Sleep) Rules
- **Permitted:** Grishma (Summer) only — 20-30 min nap recommended. Post-intense exercise: 15-20 min rest acceptable.
- **Prohibited:** Winter, Spring, Autumn, Monsoon — increases heaviness, disrupts Agni.

See [[sleep-protocols]] for full Divaswapna rules. [Source: dinacharya-rules.md]

## 8. Nocturnal Pitta: The Hidden Engine (10 PM-2 AM)

This unconscious phase is foundational to Dinacharya logic:
- Growth hormone secretion peaks (first slow-wave sleep episode)
- Hepatic detoxification enzymes (cytochrome P450) reach peak activity
- Glymphatic system activation clears neurotoxic metabolites (amyloid-beta, tau)
- Memory consolidation from prior day
- Cellular repair and autophagy

**Why evening protocol matters:** Late-night eating, alcohol, screen exposure, or emotional agitation directly impairs these repair processes. [Source: dinacharya-rules.md]

## Dinacharya — Overview and Classical Framework

Dinacharya (दिनचर्या, "daily conduct") is the Ayurvedic ideal daily routine — a stable, grounding framework of practices performed at consistent times each day. While [[ritucharya]] adapts to the macro-rhythms of the year, Dinacharya provides the micro-rhythmic stability that calms Vata Dosha (which governs the nervous system) and creates the foundation upon which seasonal adjustments are made. Health is built on the synergy of these two routines. [Source: Hindu_Months_Health_and_Wellness_full.md]

## Dinacharya × Ritucharya Synergy

The daily framework stays constant, but seasonal variables modulate it:

| Dinacharya Element | Seasonal Adaptation |
|-------------------|---------------------|
| **Abhyanga oil** | Warming sesame oil in cold/dry Vata seasons (autumn, winter) → Cooling coconut oil in hot Pitta season (summer) |
| **Exercise intensity** | Vigorous in Hemanta/Shishira (strength at peak) → Gentle in Grishma (strength depleted) |
| **Meal composition** | Heavy, nourishing in Hemanta → Light, cooling in Grishma → Unctuous, digestive in Varsha |
| **Daytime napping** | Permitted only in Grishma (energy conservation) → Contraindicated in all other seasons (increases Kapha) |
| **Bathing water** | Warm in cold seasons → Cool in summer |

## Dinacharya and the Pancha Kosha Model

Each element of the daily routine maps to one or more layers of the [[pancha-kosha]] framework:

| Dinacharya Element | Primary Kosha(s) Addressed |
|-------------------|---------------------------|
| Nutrition, Abhyanga, Sleep | **Annamaya** — physical body maintenance |
| Pranayama, Exercise, Yoga | **Pranamaya** — vital energy cultivation |
| Journaling, Meditation | **Manomaya** — emotional processing |
| Svadhyaya (study), Reflection | **Vijnanamaya** — wisdom development |
| Silent meditation, Stillness | **Anandamaya** — deep rest and contentment |

The [[pancha-kosha]] protocol sequencing logic informs Dinacharya personalisation: stabilise Annamaya (sleep, nutrition) before introducing Pranamaya practices (Pranayama), and ensure Pranamaya regulation before deeper Vijnanamaya/Anandamaya work (study, meditation). [Source: pancha_kosha_framework.md]
