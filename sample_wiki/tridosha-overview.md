The Tridosha (three doshas) doctrine is the foundational framework of Ayurvedic medicine. Vata (movement), Pitta (transformation), and Kapha (structure) are three bio-regulatory principles — not substances but patterns of intelligence and energy — that govern every physiological and psychological function in the human organism. Every individual carries all three in a unique ratio established at conception ([[prakriti-vikriti|Prakriti]]), and health is defined as maintaining that individual-specific dynamic equilibrium. [Source: Tridosha_Principle_Comprehensive_Guide.md]

## The Three Doshas at a Glance

| Dosha | Elements | Core Principle | Key Qualities | Primary Seat | Psychological Gift |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **[[vata-dosha\|Vata]]** | Akasha (Space) + Vayu (Air) | Movement | Dry, light, cold, rough, subtle, mobile | Colon (below navel) | Creativity, enthusiasm, flexibility |
| **[[pitta-dosha\|Pitta]]** | Agni (Fire) + Jala (Water) | Transformation | Hot, sharp, light, liquid, oily, spreading | Small intestine (navel to heart) | Intelligence, courage, determination |
| **[[kapha-dosha\|Kapha]]** | Jala (Water) + Prithvi (Earth) | Structure | Heavy, cool, slow, oily, smooth, stable | Chest/lungs (above heart) | Love, compassion, patience, stability |

[Source: Tridosha_Principle_Comprehensive_Guide.md]

## Elemental Foundation

The doshas arise from the [[pancha-mahabhuta|Pancha Mahabhutas (Five Great Elements)]]. Their qualities emerge not from additive combination but from synergistic interaction: Vata's mobility (from Vayu) is enabled by Akasha providing space for movement; Pitta's transformative fire (Agni) is controlled and given fluidity by Jala; Kapha's structural integrity (Prithvi) is given cohesion and lubrication by Jala. [Source: Tridosha_Principle_Comprehensive_Guide.md]

## Sub-Type Architecture (Panchavidha)

Each dosha has five sub-types (Panchavidha), creating 15 functional units that map to specific anatomical seats, physiological functions, and psychological domains:

- **[[dosha-subtypes|Vata Sub-types]]**: Prana (head/senses), Udana (throat/speech), Samana (navel/digestion), Vyana (heart/circulation), Apana (pelvis/elimination)
- **[[dosha-subtypes|Pitta Sub-types]]**: Pachaka (stomach/digestion), Ranjaka (liver/blood), Sadhaka (heart/intellect), Alochaka (eyes/vision), Bhrajaka (skin/radiance)
- **[[dosha-subtypes|Kapha Sub-types]]**: Kledaka (stomach/moisture), Avalambaka (heart-lungs/support), Bodhaka (tongue/taste), Tarpaka (brain/CSF), Sleshaka (joints/lubrication)

[Source: Vata_Dosha.md, Pitta_Dosha.md, Kapha_Dosha.md]

## Dosha Hierarchy and Interdependence

Vata is considered the "leader" or prime mover — it has the capacity to move Pitta and Kapha from their seats. When Vata becomes imbalanced, it can pull the other doshas out of equilibrium, initiating disease cascades. Pitta is the transformative dosha converting raw material into refined products. Kapha is the structural dosha providing the substrate upon which Vata acts and Pitta transforms. [Source: Tridosha_Principle_Comprehensive_Guide.md, dosha_dushya_framework.md]

## Dynamic Balance — Not Static Equilibrium

The doshas are in constant flux, responding to diet, lifestyle, emotions, seasons ([[ritucharya|Ritucharya]]), and circadian rhythms. Health is not a fixed state but a continuously maintained process. The therapeutic axiom is: **"Like increases like (Samanya); opposites balance (Vishesha)."** Aggravated Vata (dry, cold) is pacified by warm, oily qualities. Aggravated Pitta (hot, sharp) is pacified by cool, calming qualities. Aggravated Kapha (heavy, slow) is pacified by light, stimulating qualities. [Source: Tridosha_Principle_Comprehensive_Guide.md]

## Disease Theory

When doshas deviate from their natural equilibrium, they interact with vulnerable tissues (Dushyas) through the [[dosha-dushya-sammurchana|Dosha-Dushya Sammurchana]] framework, following the six-stage [[shatkriyakala|Shatkriyakala]] progression. The master upstream variable is [[agni-digestive-fire|Agni (digestive/metabolic fire)]] — its impairment produces Ama (metabolic toxin), which accelerates all pathology. [Source: dosha_dushya_framework.md]
