Nidra (sleep) is one of the four pillars of health in Ayurveda. Unlike modern sleep science, which treats sleep as monolithic, Ayurveda recognizes that sleep needs, quality, and timing vary by constitutional type and seasonal context. This page provides complete sleep protocols for each dosha, seasonal adjustments, daytime sleep rules, dream diagnosis, and sleep disorder management — the operational deep-dive companion to the sleep sections of [[dinacharya]].

## 1. Universal Principles: The Nocturnal Pitta Window

### Why 10 PM Matters

**10 PM-2 AM = Nocturnal [[pitta-dosha|Pitta]] (Internal Repair Phase):** Growth hormone secretion peaks (first slow-wave sleep), hepatic detoxification enzymes (cytochrome P450) reach peak activity, glymphatic system maximally clears neurotoxic metabolites (amyloid-beta, tau), memory consolidation and synaptic pruning occur, cellular repair and autophagy proceed. [Source: sleep-protocols.md]

**If awake past 10 PM:** Rising nocturnal Pitta creates "second wind" — increased alertness, appetite reactivation, mental activation. Pitta's energy is diverted from internal repair to external consciousness. Sleep phase delay and architecture degradation (less slow-wave sleep) follow. [Source: sleep-protocols.md]

> BRIDGE: The "forbidden zone for sleep" — late evening's elevated circadian alerting signal. Optimal sleep onset is 1-2 hours after Dim-Light Melatonin Onset (DLMO), typically 9-10 PM. [Source: sleep-protocols.md]

### Standard Sleep-Wake Timing (All Doshas)
- **Sleep window:** 10:00 PM - 6:00 AM (core)
- **Bedtime:** 10:00 PM (strict consistency)
- **Wake time:** 5:30-6:00 AM (Brahma Muhurta)
- **Duration:** 7-9 hours (varies by dosha)

[Source: sleep-protocols.md]

## 2. Vata Dosha: Sleep Protocol

### Characteristics
Light sleeper; easily disturbed by noise, temperature, movement. Tendency toward insomnia, disrupted sleep, early waking. Racing mind at bedtime (worry, planning). Vivid, often anxious dreams. [Source: sleep-protocols.md]

### Requirements

| Parameter | Vata |
|---|---|
| **Duration** | 8-9 hours (longest of all doshas) |
| **Consistency** | Extremely important; destabilizes with irregular timing |
| **Bedtime** | 9:30-10:00 PM (slightly earlier acceptable) |
| **Wake** | 5:30-6:00 AM (consistent) |
| **Naps** | Avoid; disrupts night sleep |
| **Position** | Left side preferred (calming) |

[Source: sleep-protocols.md]

### Pre-Sleep Ritual (7:00-10:00 PM)

| Time | Practice | Details |
|---|---|---|
| 8:00 PM | **Dinner** | Warm, grounding: sesame soup, warm milk with ghee, root vegetables. Sweet taste emphasized. Avoid cold, dry, stimulating, caffeine, alcohol. |
| 9:00 PM | **Environment** | Dim lighting (melatonin support), 65-68°F, remove electronics, complete darkness (blackout curtains), lavender or sandalwood aroma |
| 9:15 PM | **Pada-Abhyanga** | Warm sesame oil foot massage, 10-15 min, emphasis on soles (parasympathetic activation) |
| 9:30 PM | **Herbal Milk** | 1 cup warm milk + 1/4 tsp Ashwagandha + pinch nutmeg + honey. Alternative: Brahmi ghee, rose water |
| 9:45 PM | **[[pranayama-prescriptions\|Nadi Shodhana]]** | 5-10 min; ratio 1:4:2 (4 count inhale, 16 hold, 8 exhale). Finish with 3 rounds left nostril only (cooling, sleep-promoting). Alternative: Coherent breathing 5.5 breaths/min |
| 9:55 PM | **Meditation** | 5-10 min body scan or Yoga Nidra. Avoid mental analysis. |
| 10:00 PM | **Sleep** | Left-side position; supportive pillows; moderate blanket weight |

[Source: sleep-protocols.md]

### Sleep Environment (Non-Negotiable)
Temperature 65-68°F (cool, not cold). Complete darkness. Silence or white noise. Moderate humidity (humidifier in winter). Natural fabric bedding. Bed away from windows/doors (reduces startle). [Source: sleep-protocols.md]

### Insomnia Remedies
**If unable to sleep after 30 minutes:** Get up → cool, dark room → Nadi Shodhana or left-nostril breathing 5-10 min → warm milk with Ashwagandha → return to bed. If still unable after 30 more min: gentle restorative yoga in dim light. [Source: sleep-protocols.md]

**Chronic insomnia:** Extend pre-sleep to 2+ hours wind-down. Add afternoon Vata-pacifying yoga. Consultation for medicated milk formulations (Ashwagandha Ghrita, Brahmi Ghrita). [Source: sleep-protocols.md]

## 3. Pitta Dosha: Sleep Protocol

### Characteristics
Generally good sleepers if conditions are right. Difficulty if overheated or mind is active/driven. May wake 3-4 AM if Pitta aggravated. Intense, competitive, emotionally charged dreams. [Source: sleep-protocols.md]

### Requirements

| Parameter | Pitta |
|---|---|
| **Duration** | 7-8 hours (efficient sleeper) |
| **Consistency** | Important but slightly more flexible than Vata |
| **Bedtime** | 10:00-10:30 PM |
| **Wake** | 5:30-6:00 AM |
| **Naps** | Avoid; disrupts balance |
| **Position** | Left side preferred; right acceptable |

[Source: sleep-protocols.md]

### Pre-Sleep Ritual (7:00-10:00 PM)

| Time | Practice | Details |
|---|---|---|
| 8:00 PM | **Light Dinner** | Cooling, non-acidic: steamed vegetables, rice, ghee. Sweet/bitter taste emphasis. Avoid spicy, stimulating, alcohol, chocolate, caffeine. |
| 9:00 PM | **Gentle Yoga** | Cooling poses: forward folds, supine twists. 15-20 min. Avoid backbends (heating). |
| 9:25 PM | **Cooling Bath** | Cool (not cold) water 10-15 min. Optional rose water, jasmine, sandalwood. |
| 9:35 PM | **Cooling Tea** | Warm chamomile, Brahmi, or rose tea. Alternative: coconut milk with cardamom. |
| 9:50 PM | **Foot Massage** | Coconut oil (room temp or slightly cool), 5-10 min, light touch. |
| 9:55 PM | **Meditation** | 10 min. Cooling visualizations: moonlight, cool water. Mantra: "So Hum" or "Shanti." |
| 10:00 PM | **Sleep** | |

[Source: sleep-protocols.md]

### Sleep Environment
Temperature 65-70°F (cooler end preferred). Complete darkness. Silence or gentle nature sounds. Cooling aromas (rose, jasmine, sandalwood, lotus). Breathable cotton bedding. Away from heat sources; window access for cool air. [Source: sleep-protocols.md]

### Early Morning Waking (3-4 AM)
If waking during nocturnal Pitta: Get up → avoid stimulation → cooling herbal tea or milk with Brahmi → [[pranayama-prescriptions|Shitali or Sitkari]] pranayama → gentle moonlit walk if possible (Chandani Sevana) → return to bed after 20-30 min. [Source: sleep-protocols.md]

## 4. Kapha Dosha: Sleep Protocol

### Characteristics
Tendency toward excessive sleep (oversleeping). Deep, sound sleeper; difficult to wake. May feel groggy upon waking. Can sleep through alarms. Risk: excess sleep increases lethargy and heaviness. [Source: sleep-protocols.md]

### Requirements

| Parameter | Kapha |
|---|---|
| **Duration** | 6-7 hours (least of all doshas) |
| **Consistency** | Flexible; less critical than other doshas |
| **Bedtime** | 10:00-10:30 PM (not earlier!) |
| **Wake** | **5:30 AM (CRUCIAL; don't oversleep)** |
| **Naps** | **Strictly avoid (devastating for Kapha)** |
| **Position** | Either side; back acceptable |

[Source: sleep-protocols.md]

### Pre-Sleep Ritual (7:00-10:00 PM)

| Time | Practice | Details |
|---|---|---|
| 7:30 PM | **Early Dinner** | Light, warm, stimulating: spiced vegetable soups, light grains, warming spices. Bitter/astringent/pungent taste emphasis. Smaller portion. |
| 8:00 PM | **Vigorous Activity** | 20-30 min brisk walk, yoga, or light exercise. Prevents sluggish evening lethargy. Complete 1+ hour before sleep. |
| 8:45 PM | **[[ritucharya-lifestyle\|Udvartana]]** | Dry herbal powder massage (sandalwood, neem, chickpea flour) or dry brush. Vigorous, 10-15 min. Drying, stimulating; counteracts Kapha's heavy, oily qualities. |
| 9:00 PM | **Warm Shower** | 5-10 min. Optional stimulating herbs (ginger, neem, turmeric). |
| 9:20 PM | **Warming Tea** | Ginger, cinnamon, or turmeric tea. Avoid calming/sleep-promoting herbs (contrary to Kapha energy). |
| 9:35 PM | **Brief Reflection** | 5 min gratitude or intention-setting. Not deep meditation. |
| 9:45 PM | **Prepare Bedroom** | Bright alarm set for 5:30 AM — non-negotiable. Cool room. No extreme comfort (avoid too-soft mattress). |
| 10:00 PM | **Sleep** | Wake immediately at 5:30 AM without snoozing. |

[Source: sleep-protocols.md]

### Sleep Environment
Temperature 65-68°F (cool; Kapha tolerates cold). Standard darkness. Moderate sound acceptable. Light, natural fabric bedding; **firmer mattress** (prevents excessive burrowing). Loud alarm necessary at wake time. [Source: sleep-protocols.md]

### Oversleeping Prevention (Critical)
1. Alarm at 5:30 AM (or earlier)
2. Place alarm **across the room** (must get out of bed)
3. Use loud, stimulating alarm (not gentle)
4. Accountability partner
5. Morning activity ready (cold shower, exercise, meal prep)

**If chronic:** Cold water face wash on waking, brief cold shower, vigorous morning exercise by 7 AM, investigate potential hypothyroidism. [Source: sleep-protocols.md]

## 5. Divaswapna: Daytime Sleep Rules

### Permitted — Grishma (Summer) ONLY
Duration: 20-30 minutes maximum. Timing: 2:00-3:00 PM (peak heat). Rationale: replaces energy lost to summer heat stress. Post-nap: avoid cold exposure; gentle transition. [Source: sleep-protocols.md]

### Contraindicated
- **Winter** (Hemanta/Shishira): Increases lethargy, accumulates Kapha
- **Spring** (Vasanta): **Strictly forbidden** — aggravates Kapha Prakopa
- **Autumn** (Sharad): Disrupts Pitta recovery, interferes with Agni rebuilding
- **Monsoon** (Varsha): Depletes already-weak [[agni-digestive-fire|Agni]]
- **Post-exercise:** Brief rest (15-20 min) acceptable; full sleep problematic

[Source: sleep-protocols.md]

## 6. Dream Diagnosis by Dosha

| Dream Type | Dosha | Content | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Vata Dreams** | Vata aggravation | Flying, falling, chaotic, anxious | Nerve overstimulation |
| **Pitta Dreams** | Pitta heat | Intense, competitive, violent, fire | Digestive/metabolic stress |
| **Kapha Dreams** | Kapha stagnation | Grounded, repetitive, water-based, static | Emotional attachment |

**Remedies:** Vata nightmares → grounding pranayama, warm milk with Ashwagandha. Pitta intense dreams → cooling evening routine, Brahmi herbs. Kapha repetitive dreams → adequate daytime activity, warming herbs, shorter sleep. [Source: sleep-protocols.md]

## 7. Seasonal Sleep Adjustments

| Season | Vata | Pitta | Kapha |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Winter** (Hemanta/Shishira) | 8-9 hrs | 7-8 hrs | 6-7 hrs; resist oversleeping despite cold |
| **Spring** (Vasanta) | 8 hrs | 7 hrs | **6-6.5 hrs** (reduce to minimum; no naps) |
| **Summer** (Grishma) | 8 hrs | 7-8 hrs | 6 hrs + one afternoon nap permitted |
| **Monsoon** (Varsha) | 8 hrs | 7 hrs | 6 hrs; no naps |
| **Autumn** (Sharad) | 8 hrs (can reduce slightly) | 7 hrs | 6 hrs |

Bedtime 10:00 PM and wake 5:30-6:00 AM maintained across all seasons. See [[ritucharya]] for complete seasonal lifestyle context. [Source: sleep-protocols.md]

## 8. Sleep Disorders: Ayurvedic Perspectives

### Insomnia (Most Common: Vata Aggravation)
- **Vata insomnia:** Regular schedule, warm oils, calming herbs (Ashwagandha, Brahmi, Shatavari), Abhyanga (especially Pada-Abhyanga), Nadi Shodhana, warm milk with ghee and honey.
- **Pitta insomnia:** Cooling evening routine, cool environment, herbs (Brahmi, Jatamamsi), cool bath, coconut oil massage.
- **Kapha paradoxical insomnia (lethargy):** Early rising (non-negotiable), morning cold exposure, vigorous activity, Udvartana, stimulating herbs (ginger, black pepper, turmeric).

[Source: sleep-protocols.md]

### Sleep Apnea
Typically Kapha-[[pitta-dosha|Pitta]] combination (obstructed airways, heavy tissues). Support alongside medical care: nasal cleansing (Neti, Nasya), warming herbs (ginger, turmeric, black pepper), avoid heavy evening meals, left-side sleep, weight management. [Source: sleep-protocols.md]
