Kapha is one of the three fundamental constitutional principles (doshas) in Ayurveda, composed of Jala (Water) and Prithvi (Earth). The term derives from Sanskrit meaning "to embrace" or "to hold together." Kapha is the principle of structure, cohesion, stability, lubrication, and nourishment — the body's primary anabolic force responsible for growth, tissue development, repair, and immune protection. It acts as the body's "biological glue," holding cells and tissues together. [Source: Kapha_Dosha.md, Tridosha_Principle_Comprehensive_Guide.md]

## Elemental Composition and Qualities (Gunas)

**Elements**: Jala (Water) + Prithvi (Earth) — Water provides cohesion, lubrication, and fluidity; Earth provides structure, stability, and density. [Source: Tridosha_Principle_Comprehensive_Guide.md]

| Quality (Guna) | Sanskrit | Manifestation                                          |
| -------------- | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Heavy          | Guru     | Heavy body build, groundedness; lethargy when excess   |
| Cool/Cold      | Shita    | Cool skin, calm temperament, preference for warmth     |
| Slow/Dull      | Manda    | Slow movements, slow digestion, methodical approach    |
| Oily/Unctuous  | Snigdha  | Smooth skin, lubricated joints, moist mucous membranes |
| Smooth         | Slakshna | Smooth skin, smooth functioning of systems             |
| Stable/Steady  | Sthira   | Physical stability, mental calmness, endurance         |
| Dense/Sticky   | Picchila | Binds structures; cloudiness of mind when excess       |

## Body Type (Kapha Prakriti)

- Solid, sturdy, well-built frame
- Strong, well-developed muscles and body mass
- Large, soft, slow-moving eyes
- Thick, dark, lustrous hair
- Oily, smooth, pale complexion
- Slow metabolism, tendency to weight gain
- Excellent endurance and physical strength
- Cool body temperature

[Source: Kapha_Dosha.md]

## Physiological Functions

- **Structure** — physical form, substance to tissues and organs
- **Lubrication** (Snehana) — joints, mucous membranes, digestive tract
- **Nourishment** (Tarpana) — all bodily tissues
- **Immunity** (Bala) — protection, resilience against disease
- **Stability** and strength — physical and mental
- **Fluid balance** — cerebrospinal fluid, synovial fluid, plasma
- **Growth and development** — tissue formation, repair

Kapha's immunity function is intimately connected with **Ojas** — the finest essence of all dhatus, the master integrative substance governing immune competence, vitality, and psychological stability. Strong Kapha supports healthy Ojas formation. [Source: Kapha_Dosha.md, Tridosha_Principle_Comprehensive_Guide.md, dosha_dushya_framework.md]

## Psychological Functions (Balanced)

- Love, compassion, empathy
- Patience, forgiveness, contentment
- Mental stability, calmness, groundedness
- Sense of security and devotion
- Good long-term memory

[Source: Kapha_Dosha.md, Tridosha_Principle_Comprehensive_Guide.md]

## Primary Locations (Sthana)

**Main seats**: Chest/lungs (Uras) and stomach (Amashaya) — above the heart. Also: throat, head/sinuses, pancreas, lymphatic system (Rasa dhatu), adipose tissue (Meda dhatu), nose, tongue, joints. The stomach is particularly important as the seat of Kledaka Kapha, which moistens food and protects the stomach lining — but excess Kapha here dampens Agni, producing Ama from the first stages of digestion. [Source: Tridosha_Principle_Comprehensive_Guide.md]

## Imbalances (Kapha Vikara)

**Mental/Emotional**: Depression, lethargy, attachment, possessiveness, resistance to change, mental fog, lack of motivation, apathy, excessive sleep, emotional numbness. [Source: Kapha_Dosha.md, dosha_dushya_framework.md]

**Physical**: Weight gain/obesity, sluggish metabolism, congestion, excess mucus, cough/colds, water retention/edema, joint stiffness, high cholesterol, sinus issues, respiratory issues (asthma, bronchitis), tumors, enlarged organs. [Source: Kapha_Dosha.md, Tridosha_Principle_Comprehensive_Guide.md]

**Aggravating factors**: Cold/wet/heavy/sweet/oily foods, cold/damp weather, excessive sleep, sedentary behaviour, heavy foods/overeating, excessive dairy/wheat, daytime sleeping, emotional comfort-seeking through food. [Source: Kapha_Dosha.md, Tridosha_Principle_Comprehensive_Guide.md]

## Balancing Approaches

See [[tridosha-balancing-protocols]] for full protocols.

- **Tastes to favor**: Pungent, Bitter, Astringent
- **Diet**: Light, dry, warming foods; warming spices (ginger, black pepper, turmeric); legumes, barley, millet; minimal oil
- **Lifestyle**: Vigorous regular exercise, variety, stimulation, warmth/dryness, avoid daytime sleep, dry brushing (Garshana)
- **Herbs**: Ginger, Black Pepper, Turmeric, Trikatu (Ginger+Black Pepper+Long Pepper), Guggul, Bibhitaki
- **Pranayama**: Bhastrika (bellows breath), Kapalabhati

[Source: Kapha_Dosha.md, Tridosha_Principle_Comprehensive_Guide.md]

## Seasonal Cycle

- **Accumulation (Chaya)**: Hemanta/Shishira (Winter) — cold, damp, heavy conditions
- **Aggravation (Prakopa)**: Vasanta (Spring) — accumulated Kapha liquefies like melting snow
- **Pacification (Prashama)**: Grishma (Summer) and parts of Sharad (Autumn) — warmth and dryness

See [[ritucharya]] for prevention protocols. [Source: Tridosha_Principle_Comprehensive_Guide.md]

## NeoRishi Application

- **VBR Engine**: Kapha represents structure, nourishment, and embodied stability; assessment identifies individuals with strong structural capacity, good stamina, and natural stability who benefit from stimulating, activating practices
- **SPANDA Quest**: Kapha types benefit from stimulating, movement-based, challenging quests that promote metabolic activity; they excel in endurance and long-term commitment but need practices promoting lightness and change
- **PanchaKosha**: Kapha's structural quality directly supports [[annamaya-kosha|Annamaya Kosha]] (physical sheath) as foundation for all other sheaths
- **BioRhythm**: Kapha time = 6-10 AM/PM — vigorous exercise, complex mental work, lighter meals (morning); light dinner, calming activities, early sleep (evening)
- **Ahara Pillar**: Light, warming, pungent/bitter/astringent food recommendations
- **Vihara Pillar**: Vigorous exercise, variety, warmth, no daytime naps
