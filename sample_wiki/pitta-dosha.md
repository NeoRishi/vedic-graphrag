Pitta is one of the three fundamental constitutional principles (doshas) in Ayurveda, composed primarily of Agni (Fire) with Jala (Water) as a secondary element. The term derives from Sanskrit meaning "to burn" or "to digest." Pitta is the principle of transformation, metabolism, and enzymatic action — governing all heat-producing, digestive, and biochemical conversion processes. It is the body's chief metabolic operator, responsible for converting food into energy, sensory input into perception, and experience into understanding. [Source: Pitta_Dosha.md, Tridosha_Principle_Comprehensive_Guide.md]

## Elemental Composition and Qualities (Gunas)

**Elements**: Agni (Fire) + Jala (Water) — Fire's transformative power is modulated and contained by Water, preventing unchecked combustion. [Source: Tridosha_Principle_Comprehensive_Guide.md]

| Quality (Guna) | Sanskrit | Manifestation |
|---|---|---|
| Hot | Ushna | Body warmth, strong digestion, preference for coolness |
| Sharp/Penetrating | Tikshna | Sharp intellect, keen perception, sharp hunger |
| Liquid | Drava | Digestive juices, blood, spreading tendency |
| Light | Laghu | Active, intense nature |
| Slightly Oily | Snigdha | Tissue protection from Fire's intensity |
| Spreading | Sara | Tendency to spread quickly (like fire or liquid) |
| Acidic/Pungent | Amla/Katu | Associated tastes indicating Pitta state |

[Source: Tridosha_Principle_Comprehensive_Guide.md]

## Body Type (Pitta Prakriti)

- Medium, well-proportioned build with defined musculature
- Fair to reddish complexion, often with freckles or moles
- Sharp, piercing eyes (often green, gray, or hazel)
- Premature graying or balding
- Warm body temperature, efficient metabolism
- Strong appetite and good digestion
- Tendency toward excessive heat and perspiration
- Moderate to oily skin, prone to inflammation

[Source: Pitta_Dosha.md]

## Physiological Functions

- **Digestion** (Pachana) — breakdown, absorption, assimilation of nutrients
- **Metabolism** at all levels — cellular to gross
- **Temperature regulation** (Ushma) and thermogenesis
- **Vision** (Darshana) — eyes governed by Alochaka Pitta
- **Hormonal production** and regulation
- **Enzymatic reactions** and biochemical transformations
- **Blood formation** and oxygenation
- **Skin pigmentation** and luster
- **Mental clarity** and intellectual function

[Source: Pitta_Dosha.md, Tridosha_Principle_Comprehensive_Guide.md]

## Psychological Functions (Balanced)

- Sharp intellect, quick comprehension, discernment (Buddhi, Medha)
- Courage (Shaurya), determination (Dhairya), ambition
- Natural leadership, focused mind, effective decision-making
- Capacity to "digest" experiences — processing information and integrating understanding

[Source: Pitta_Dosha.md, Tridosha_Principle_Comprehensive_Guide.md]

## Primary Locations (Sthana)

**Main seat**: Small intestine (Grahani) and stomach (Amashaya) — between navel and heart. Also: liver (Yakrut), spleen (Pliha), gallbladder (Pittashaya), blood (Rakta dhatu), sweat glands, subcutaneous fat, eyes (Netra). The eyes are a diagnostic window — redness, inflammation, or yellowish discoloration indicates Pitta aggravation, connected to liver health. [Source: Tridosha_Principle_Comprehensive_Guide.md]

## Imbalances (Pitta Vikara)

**Mental/Emotional**: Anger, irritability, hostility, jealousy, criticism, perfectionism, impatience, competitive drive, workaholism, burnout. [Source: Pitta_Dosha.md, dosha_dushya_framework.md]

**Physical**: Inflammation, hyperacidity, acid reflux, heartburn, skin rashes/acne/eczema, loose stools/diarrhea, excessive sweating, fever, eye strain, bleeding disorders, ulcers, high blood pressure. [Source: Pitta_Dosha.md, Tridosha_Principle_Comprehensive_Guide.md]

**Aggravating factors**: Hot/humid weather, spicy/oily/fermented foods, excessive sun, stress/competition, overwork, alcohol, sour/salty/pungent tastes, late-night activities. [Source: Pitta_Dosha.md, Tridosha_Principle_Comprehensive_Guide.md]

## Pitta's Duality

Pitta embodies constructive-destructive duality: the same fire that digests food and illuminates intellect can, when excessive, inflame tissues and produce burnout. The Water element within Pitta is the critical moderator — when insufficient, unchecked fire becomes destructive. [Source: Tridosha_Principle_Comprehensive_Guide.md]

## Balancing Approaches

See [[tridosha-balancing-protocols]] for full protocols.

- **Tastes to favor**: Sweet, Bitter, Astringent
- **Diet**: Cool, refreshing foods; coconut, cucumber, leafy greens, barley; ghee and coconut oil
- **Lifestyle**: Moderation in all activities, coolness, avoid excess heat/sun, stress management, non-competitive exercise
- **Herbs**: Amalaki (cooling), Shatavari (rejuvenating), Brahmi, Neem, Aloe Vera, Sandalwood
- **Pranayama**: Shitali, Sitkari (cooling breaths)

[Source: Pitta_Dosha.md, Tridosha_Principle_Comprehensive_Guide.md]

## Seasonal Cycle

- **Accumulation (Chaya)**: Varsha (Monsoon) — acidic conditions, weakened Agni
- **Aggravation (Prakopa)**: Sharad (Autumn) — return of heat after rains; also Grishma (Summer)
- **Pacification (Prashama)**: Hemanta/Shishira (Winter) — cooling seasons

See [[ritucharya]] for prevention protocols. [Source: Tridosha_Principle_Comprehensive_Guide.md]

## NeoRishi Application

- **VBR Engine**: Pitta represents transformation and metabolic intelligence; assessment identifies individuals with strong transformative capacity, high metabolism, and intellectual acuity
- **SPANDA Quest**: Pitta types excel in analytical, transformative, goal-oriented quest phases but need cooling practices to prevent burnout and over-intensity
- **PanchaKosha**: Pitta's transformative quality corresponds to digesting experiences across [[annamaya-kosha|Annamaya Kosha]] (physical) through [[vijnanamaya-kosha|Vijnanamaya Kosha]] (wisdom) sheaths
- **BioRhythm**: Pitta time = 10 AM-2 PM / 10 PM-2 AM — largest meal and focused work (day); deep sleep for tissue repair (night)
- **Ahara Pillar**: Cool, sweet/bitter/astringent food recommendations
- **Vihara Pillar**: Moderation, coolness, non-competitive exercise
